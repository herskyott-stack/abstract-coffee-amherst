Roastery & café · Amherst, NS

Roasted here. Poured here.

Abstract Coffee is Amherst’s own coffee roastery and café on South Albion Street — small-batch beans roasted in-house, a full espresso bar, and a seasonal menu that changes with what tastes good right now. Come for a flat white; leave with a bag of beans for home.

147 South Albion St, Amherst, NS · Mon–Fri 8–4, Sat 9–2 · Follow the daily drinks on Facebook

The Roastery

Beans roasted in small batches right in the shop — fresh for the espresso bar and bagged for home brewing. Ask what’s on the roaster this week.

Espresso Bar

Lattes, cappuccinos, flat whites and the classics, made on beans we roasted ourselves. Dairy or oat — your call.

Seasonal Specials

The menu board rotates: a True North Fog, a crème brûlée latte, a cinnamon roll fog, maple cold brew. When it’s gone, it’s gone.

Beans To Go

Take the café home — retail bags of the current roasts, ground how you like. The freshest coffee in Cumberland County, by the bag.
